Best A24 Horror Films, Ranked

No studio has reshaped modern horror quite like A24. Since the mid-2010s, the distributor has championed films that trade cheap thrills for slow-building psychological dread, turning grief, folklore, and existential isolation into the genre's most potent raw materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes A24 horror different from mainstream horror?
A24 horror films prioritize atmosphere, character psychology, and slow-building dread over jump scares and gore. They use horror as a lens for exploring grief, trauma, isolation, and identity — resulting in films that linger long after the credits. Directors like Ari Aster and Robert Eggers have become synonymous with this approach.
What was the first A24 horror film?
A24's first major horror release was Under the Skin (2013), starring Scarlett Johansson. However, the studio's horror identity crystallized with The Witch (2015), which became a breakout hit and established A24 as the go-to distributor for auteur-driven horror.
Are A24 horror films actually scary?
A24 horror films are often more disturbing than conventionally scary. Rather than jump scares, they create persistent unease through atmosphere and psychological tension. Films like Hereditary and Talk to Me deliver intense visceral scares, while The Lighthouse and Under the Skin operate on existential dread.
Where can I stream A24 horror movies?
A24 horror films are across multiple platforms. Paramount+ carries the widest selection. Apple TV+ has Hereditary and Midsommar. Prime Video hosts The Lighthouse and Lamb. Others are on Hulu, Max, Shudder, Showtime, and Tubi. Availability changes regularly.
