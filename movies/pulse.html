The vibe

Kiyoshi Kurosawa's internet-age ghost story reads dial-up loneliness as an apocalypse: spirits seeping through screens because the afterlife is as isolating as this one. Almost no jump scares, just smudged figures in doorways and a sadness that swallows Tokyo whole.

Before you press play

How scary is Pulse?
On our intensity scale, Pulse rates 2/5 for scares and 5/5 for dread. It is on the gentler side — it favors mood and atmosphere over hard scares. Expect little in the way of jump scares, but a sense of dread that builds steadily and lingers after the credits.
