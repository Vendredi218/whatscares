The vibe

The definitive jiangshi film: a Taoist priest and two hapless disciples manage a hopping corpse with sticky rice, ink lines, and held breath. Lam Ching-ying's unibrowed priest became an icon. Slapstick kung-fu horror that spawned an entire genre.
