The vibe

Bayona's Spanish ghost story runs on maternal terror: a woman returns to the seaside orphanage where she grew up, and her son starts talking to a boy in a sack mask. The knocking game is an all-timer scene, and the ending trades scares for heartbreak that lingers longer.

Before you press play

How scary is The Orphanage?
On our intensity scale, The Orphanage rates 3/5 for scares and 5/5 for dread. It is moderately scary, with a handful of strong sequences. Expect little in the way of jump scares, but a sense of dread that builds steadily and lingers after the credits.
