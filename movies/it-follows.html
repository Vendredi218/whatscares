The vibe

A shape only you can see walks toward you forever, passed on like a curse. Mitchell's premise is elegantly simple and infinitely dreadful — wide shots become threat assessments, and Disasterpeace's synth score turns suburban Detroit into a haunted dream.

Before you press play

How scary is It Follows?
On our intensity scale, It Follows rates 3/5 for scares and 5/5 for dread. It is moderately scary, with a handful of strong sequences. Expect little in the way of jump scares, but a sense of dread that builds steadily and lingers after the credits.
How gory is It Follows?
It Follows rates 1/5 for gore — expect almost no gore.
Where can I stream It Follows?
As of our last update, It Follows is streaming on Paramount+ and Tubi in the US. Availability changes, so check your service before settling in.
